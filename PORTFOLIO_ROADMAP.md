# Research portfolio roadmap

Prioritize one validated project over several unfinished repositories. These are proposed directions based on 2D-material spectroscopy and device research, not claims of completed work.

## 1. Spectroscopy analysis — provided starter

Reproduce every output and explain the Gaussian model and uncertainty assumptions. Add a meaningful contribution yourself: for example, Gaussian-versus-Voigt comparison on simulated spectra with known broadening, evaluated through residuals and parameter recovery. Record what you changed and learned.

Then add a shareable experimental spectrum, if available, with acquisition conditions, calibration, units, provenance, and permission to publish. Retain the synthetic benchmark. Do not present simulated results as experimental work.

## 2. Temperature-dependent emission — proposed

Use a concrete dataset or simulation question. Fit emission energy shifts, examine parameter identifiability, and document uncertainty and model assumptions. An intensity-quenching activation energy alone does not uniquely identify a mechanism. Deliver reusable analysis, provenance, figures, and a short interpretation.

## 3. Device or optical modeling — proposed

Choose one bounded question connected to work you can explain, such as resonance sensitivity to geometry. State material constants, boundary conditions, and solver limitations. Validate against an analytic limiting case or trusted benchmark before interpreting results. Avoid adding simulations merely to increase repository count.

## Profile presentation

Pin projects after verifying that they run from a clean environment. Each README should explain the question, method, data source, result, uncertainty, limitations, and your contribution. Use only confirmed skills and current biographical details in a future profile README. Publish unpublished work only with appropriate authority.
